The Setup

A subscriber wants to pay. The contract needs to store the payment amount, whether they're active, and their wallet address.

Then it needs to check: did they send enough? Your job is to pick the right types and complete the validation function.

+ Hint
Payment amounts are whole numbers that can never be negative — and in Solana they're measured in lamports, which can reach into the billions. Which integer type handles that? And does "valid" mean strictly greater than, or at least equal to?
